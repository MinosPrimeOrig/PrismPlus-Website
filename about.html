About

Prism+ is a community-driven Minecraft launcher by Minos_Team.

Mission

Keep what makes Prism great, and add what players and server owners are missing, without a rebuild from scratch and without risking anyone's data.

Credits

Prism Launcher is the foundation of Prism+. Thanks to its authors and contributors.

Selected ideas from Nivora Launcher are re-implemented independently, with respect for its license.

Independent project

Prism+ is an independent community project. It is not an official Prism Launcher release.
